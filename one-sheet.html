Songwriter · Huntsville, Alabama

Ken Mackenzie

Real People, Real Emotions

Kenmack Music, LLC · ASCAP · kenmackmusic.com

I write songs about people and the moments that change them, in styles ranging from Americana and country to folk, rock, indie, and pop. Years of hospital work put me inside some of the most extreme human conditions, with people having their best days and their worst, and gave me a depth and range of emotional experience that colors everything I write. My vocal training shapes the writing, so the words sing as naturally as they read. My lyrics are open to artists, producers, co-writers, and collaborators.
